## 核心内容

Locating Underwater Locator Beacons (ULBs) in deep-sea conditions is still a significant challenge due to the severe Attenuation (acoustic) and complex multipath transmission, and the presence of high ambient ocean noise. Current localization methods tend to be based on simplified acoustic propagation models or computationally intensive physics-based solvers, thus limiting the accuracy of localization and the ability to deploy it in real-time in practical Search and Rescue (SAR) missions...

## 关键技术与数据

提出物理信息混合海洋捕食者-哈里斯鹰优化算法（PIMPHHO），用于深海水下定位信标（ULB）定位，结合被动声呐建模，将声传播衰减、多径效应与环境噪声纳入优化目标，通过混合元启发式算法搜索最优声源位置。

## 结果与结论

该方法在深水强衰减、复杂多径与高噪声条件下定位精度优于传统简化传播模型和纯物理求解器方法，且计算效率满足实时搜救需求，为深水黑匣子定位提供了鲁棒且可部署的优化框架。
