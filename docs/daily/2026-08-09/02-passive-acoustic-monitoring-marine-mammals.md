# Passive Acoustic Monitoring of Marine Mammals Using Hydrophone Arrays

## 摘要

This study presents a comprehensive passive acoustic monitoring system for detecting and classifying marine mammal vocalizations using large-aperture hydrophone arrays. The proposed beamforming and classification algorithms achieve high detection rates even in low SNR conditions.
