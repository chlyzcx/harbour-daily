# Deep Learning-Based Underwater Acoustic Channel Estimation for OFDM Systems

## 摘要

This paper proposes a novel deep learning-based channel estimation method for underwater acoustic OFDM systems. The proposed approach leverages convolutional neural networks to capture the time-varying characteristics of underwater acoustic channels, achieving significant performance improvement over traditional estimation methods.
