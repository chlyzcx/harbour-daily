# Development of Efficient Wavelet Scheme in DWT, FFT &amp; DTCWT based OFDM Model for Underwater Communication

## 摘要

The multipath propagation, Doppler spread, and channel distortion have negative impacts on the reliability of Underwater acoustic communication channels. Therefore, OFDM communication becomes unreliable because of these impairments.
